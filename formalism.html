Formalism 1. The postulates

The rules are compact; the consequences are large.

A pure state is a normalized vector \(|\psi\rangle\) in a complex Hilbert space. Observables are represented by Hermitian operators. If \(\hat A|a_n\rangle=a_n|a_n\rangle\), then measuring \(A\) returns one of the eigenvalues \(a_n\), with probability \(|\langle a_n|\psi\rangle|^2\).

Time evolution is generated by the Hamiltonian. For a time-independent Hamiltonian, the formal solution is unitary: \( |\psi(t)\rangle=e^{-i\hat Ht/\hbar}|\psi(0)\rangle \).

Normalization

Probability one means \[ \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\,dx = 1. \] If \(\Psi\) is multiplied by a constant, normalization fixes the allowed constant.

Expectation values

The average result over repeated preparations is \[ \langle A\rangle = \langle\psi|\hat A|\psi\rangle. \] In position space, \(\langle x\rangle=\int x|\Psi(x,t)|^2dx\).

Compatible measurements

Operators commute when there is a common eigenbasis: \[ [\hat A,\hat B]=0. \] Nonzero commutators are the algebraic source of uncertainty relations.

\[ \hat A=\sum_n a_n P_n,\qquad P_n=|a_n\rangle\langle a_n|,\qquad P_mP_n=\delta_{mn}P_n. \] \[ p(a_n)=\langle\psi|P_n|\psi\rangle=|\langle a_n|\psi\rangle|^2,\qquad \langle A\rangle=\sum_n a_n p(a_n). \]

Hermiticity is what makes measurement results real and makes the eigenvectors usable as an orthonormal coordinate system for probabilities.

\[ |\psi\rangle=c_1|e_1\rangle+c_2|e_2\rangle,\qquad c'_i=\langle e'_i|\psi\rangle,\qquad c'=U^\dagger c. \] \[ \hat A'=U^\dagger\hat A U,\qquad \langle\psi|\hat A|\psi\rangle=c^\dagger A c=c'^\dagger A'c'. \]

Matrix mechanics is bookkeeping for this invariance. The column vector and matrix entries change with the chosen basis; probabilities and expectation values do not.

\[ I=\sum_n |e_n\rangle\langle e_n|,\qquad |\psi\rangle=I|\psi\rangle=\sum_n |e_n\rangle\langle e_n|\psi\rangle. \] \[ \sum_n |c_n|^2=1,\qquad \langle A\rangle=\sum_{m,n}c_m^*A_{mn}c_n. \]

Completeness is the bridge between abstract kets and concrete calculations. It lets us insert a convenient basis anywhere, then compute amplitudes, probabilities, and matrix elements.

\[ B(\alpha)=\cos\alpha\,\sigma_z+\sin\alpha\,\sigma_x,\qquad [\sigma_z,B(\alpha)]=2i\sin\alpha\,\sigma_y. \] \[ [A,B]=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{\(A\) and \(B\) can be diagonalized in the same orthonormal basis.} \]

The commutator is not just notation. It decides which questions can have sharp answers in the same prepared state and which sequential measurements must be treated as physically different experiments.

\[ |\psi(t)\rangle=U(t)|\psi(0)\rangle,\qquad U(t)=e^{-i\hat Ht/\hbar},\qquad U^\dagger U=I. \] \[ |\psi(t)\rangle=\sum_n c_n e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}|E_n\rangle,\qquad |c_n(t)|^2=|c_n(0)|^2. \]

Energy measurements are stationary in a time-independent Hamiltonian, but any observable that is not diagonal in the energy basis can oscillate because it compares components with different phases.

\[ |\psi_S(t)\rangle=U(t)|\psi(0)\rangle,\qquad A_H(t)=U^\dagger(t)A_SU(t). \] \[ \langle\psi_S(t)|A_S|\psi_S(t)\rangle =\langle\psi(0)|A_H(t)|\psi(0)\rangle. \] \[ \frac{dA_H}{dt}=\frac{i}{\hbar}[H,A_H]+\left(\frac{\partial A}{\partial t}\right)_H. \]

These are not different physical theories. They are two coordinate choices for the same transition amplitudes and expectation values.

\[ \frac{d}{dt}\langle A\rangle =\frac{i}{\hbar}\langle[\hat H,\hat A]\rangle +\left\langle\frac{\partial \hat A}{\partial t}\right\rangle. \] \[ \frac{d}{dt}\langle x\rangle=\frac{\langle p\rangle}{m},\qquad \frac{d}{dt}\langle p\rangle=-\left\langle\frac{dV}{dx}\right\rangle. \] \[ \frac{d}{dt}\langle H\rangle=0 \quad \text{when } \partial \hat H/\partial t=0. \]

Conservation laws come from symmetries: if an observable commutes with \(\hat H\), its expectation is constant in time.
